Our Philosophy

More Than a Gym.
A Family. A Legacy.

At Alers Martial Arts, we believe martial arts transforms lives — not just bodies. Founded by professional fighter Jim Alers and his wife Melisa, AMA has been the premier martial arts destination in Pembroke Pines for over 15 years.

From your very first class to the competition mat, our coaches bring out the champion in every student — regardless of age, experience, or fitness level.

  • BJJ Black Belt instruction
  • Professional MMA competition experience
  • Safe, family-friendly environment
  • Flexible schedules for all lifestyles
  • Award-winning coaching staff

Meet the Team

Coached by Champions

JIM ALERS
Head Coach

Jim "The Beast" Alers

Head Coach & Co-Founder

BJJ Black Belt 20 Pro MMA Fights 4-1 Bare Knuckle UCF Graduate 15+ Years Teaching

Jim "The Beast" Alers brings elite professional fighting experience to every single class. With 20 professional MMA fights and a 4-1 bare knuckle record, Jim's real-world competition knowledge is unmatched in South Florida. As a BJJ Black Belt and UCF graduate, he combines technical mastery with 15+ years of dedicated teaching to help every student — from first-day beginners to seasoned competitors — reach their true potential.
