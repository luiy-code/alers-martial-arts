Our Philosophy

More Than a Gym.
A Family. A Legacy.

At Alers Martial Arts, we believe martial arts transforms lives — not just bodies. Founded by professional fighter Jim Alers and his wife Melisa, AMA has been the premier martial arts destination in Pembroke Pines for over 15 years.

From your very first class to the competition mat, our coaches bring out the champion in every student — regardless of age, experience, or fitness level.

  • BJJ Black Belt instruction
  • Professional MMA competition experience
  • Safe, family-friendly environment
  • Flexible schedules for all lifestyles
  • Award-winning coaching staff

Before You Train

Liability Waiver

Please complete the digital waiver before your first class. This is a one-time requirement for all students and their parents/guardians.

ALERS MARTIAL ARTS — LIABILITY WAIVER & RELEASE FORM

Read carefully before signing. This is a legally binding agreement.

Assumption of Risk

I understand and acknowledge that martial arts training, including but not limited to Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, MMA, Kickboxing, Muay Thai, Boxing, and Fitness classes, involves risk of physical injury. I voluntarily assume all risks associated with participation in classes, open mats, drills, sparring, and any activities at Alers Martial Arts (AMA) facilities located at 911 NW 209th Ave Suite 108, Pembroke Pines, FL 33029 and 15620 SW 29th St #201, Miramar, FL 33027.

Release of Liability

In consideration of being permitted to participate in AMA programs, I, on behalf of myself, my heirs, assigns, and legal representatives, hereby release, waive, discharge, and covenant not to sue Alers Martial Arts, its owners, instructors, employees, agents, and affiliates from any and all liability, claims, demands, actions, or causes of action arising out of or related to any injury, loss, or damage suffered by me during or related to participation in AMA activities.

Medical Authorization

I certify that I am in good physical condition and have no medical condition that would prevent my safe participation in martial arts training. In case of emergency, I authorize AMA staff to seek emergency medical care on my behalf and release all medical personnel from liability for any treatment given.

Photo & Video Consent

I grant Alers Martial Arts permission to photograph and/or video record me during training and events, and to use such media for promotional purposes including social media, website, and print materials.

Rules & Conduct

I agree to follow all gym rules, instructor instructions, and to treat all members and staff with respect. I understand that unsafe conduct may result in removal from the program.

For students under 18: A parent or legal guardian must sign below.

Type your full legal name to sign electronically. By typing your name, you agree this constitutes a legal electronic signature.

Meet Your Coaches

Every instructor at Alers Martial Arts is a practitioner first — we compete, we train, we live the art. That shared passion is what separates AMA from the rest.

Head Coach

Jim "The Beast" Alers

Head Coach & Co-Founder

BJJ Black Belt UCF Graduate
15+ Years Teaching
20 Pro MMA Fights
4-1 Bare Knuckle
500+ Students Trained

Jim "The Beast" Alers isn't just a coach — he's a proven professional fighter who has competed at the highest levels of MMA and bare knuckle boxing. With 20 professional MMA bouts and a 4-1 bare knuckle record, Jim brings an authentic, hard-earned perspective to every class he teaches.

As a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Black Belt and UCF graduate, Jim has spent over 15 years refining his coaching methodology. Whether you're stepping on the mat for the very first time or prepping for your next fight, Jim has the technical depth and real-world experience to get you there.

AMA was built on his belief that every person — regardless of age, background, or fitness level — deserves elite martial arts instruction in a family-first environment.
